50 Logical Reasoning Questions and Expected Results

Question format note:
Each item gives explicit premises followed by one yes/no question. The expected
result uses ENTAILED, CONTRADICTED, UNKNOWN, or INCONSISTENT. The solver is
intended to use only the premises in the question, not outside knowledge.

Question: All cats are mammals. Tom is a cat. Is Tom a mammal?
Answer: ENTAILED - Yes. The rule that all cats are mammals applies to Tom.

12.

13.

14.

Answer: ENTAILED - Meera is a scientist, a recognized expert, and therefore a database user.
